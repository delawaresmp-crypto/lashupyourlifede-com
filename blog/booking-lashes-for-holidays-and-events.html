Booking lashes for holidays and big events in Delaware

Holiday parties, family photos and weddings fill the calendar fast. Here is how to plan your lashes around them.

The busiest times for lashes are the times you want them most. Holidays, family photos, weddings and vacations all stack up, and appointment slots go quickly.

Plan backward from the date

Pick your event date, then count back. You want a fresh fill two to four days before the event. That gives the lashes time to settle so they look natural and full, not brand new and stiff.

New to lashes? Start earlier

If you have never had extensions, book your first full set a few weeks before the event. That way you know how your eyes react and you can adjust the style at your fill.

Family photos

For photos, many people go a little fuller than their everyday look. Ask Kelsey about adding some volume for the shoot and going back to your usual style at the next fill.

Holiday travel

Lashes are great for travel because you wake up ready. Book your fill right before you leave. Pack a spoolie and a lash safe cleanser.

Prefer something lower key?

A lash lift and tint gives you curl and color with no fills. It is a nice option if you want to look polished for the season without ongoing appointments.
