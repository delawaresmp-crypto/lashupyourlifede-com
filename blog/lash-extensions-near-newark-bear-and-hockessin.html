Lash extensions near Newark, Bear and Hockessin

Looking for a lash artist in New Castle County? The Delaware SMP studio in Wilmington is a short drive from Newark, Bear, Hockessin and Pike Creek.

If you live in New Castle County, you do not need to drive to Philadelphia for good lashes. Kelsey Tyre works out of the Delaware SMP studio at 2601 Annand Drive, Unit 16, Wilmington, DE 19808, which is an easy trip from most of the county.

Easy to reach from

  • Pike Creek and Prices Corner: just a few minutes away.
  • Newark: a short drive up Route 2 or Route 7.
  • Hockessin and Greenville: a quick trip down Route 41 or 141.
  • Bear and New Castle: straight up Route 7 or I-95.
  • Middletown: about 30 minutes on Route 1.

Why a nearby artist matters for lashes

Extensions need fills every two to three weeks. If your artist is an hour away, fills get skipped and your set falls apart. Picking someone close to home or work makes it easy to keep your lashes full.

What Kelsey offers

  • Classic, hybrid and volume lash extensions
  • Lash lift and tint
  • Brows and microblading
